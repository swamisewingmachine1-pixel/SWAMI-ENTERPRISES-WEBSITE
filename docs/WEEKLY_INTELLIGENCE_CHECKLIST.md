# Weekly Intelligence Checklist

Review `/admin/intelligence` and `/admin/seo` once a week. Takes 10
minutes. Check these 12 things, in this order:

1. **Traffic** — check Vercel Analytics dashboard directly (not
   `/admin/intelligence` — it deliberately doesn't show a visitor count,
   see `docs/INTELLIGENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md` for why).
2. **Top products** (`/admin/intelligence` → Product demand) — which
   machines are actually getting viewed/quoted/won.
3. **Top sources** — which channel (Instagram, Google, direct, etc.) is
   actually producing leads, not just clicks.
4. **Top cities** — where real leads are coming from.
5. **WhatsApp clicks** (Funnel section) — real count.
6. **Phone clicks** — real count.
7. **Quote starts** — how many people began the form vs how many
   submitted (a big gap here means the form itself has friction).
8. **Leads** — total real count.
9. **Qualified leads** — the number that actually matters more than
   raw lead count.
10. **Follow-ups** (`/admin/intelligence` → Follow-ups needed) — anyone
    overdue right now.
11. **Customer questions** (`/admin/seo`) — any new real ones logged
    this week? Any ready to move from UNVALIDATED to VALIDATED?
12. **Prospect activity** (`/admin/seo`) — any prospects due for
    follow-up.

## The one rule that matters most

**Do not optimize for traffic alone.** A week with more visitors and the
same number of qualified leads is not a good week. Priority order:

```
QUALIFIED LEADS → QUOTATIONS → ORDERS → REVENUE
```

If traffic goes up but qualified leads don't, the problem is on the
website (wrong content, wrong CTA, wrong audience) — not something more
traffic will fix.

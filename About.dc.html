About the shop

A neighbourhood sewing
machine dealer since 2015

Swami Enterprises has traded on 30 Feet Road in Chanakya Place, Delhi for over a decade, run by proprietor Mr. Dinesh Jaglain. What started as a small counter selling domestic machines has grown into a full stock of domestic and industrial sewing machines, motors and spare parts — serving home tailors and small garment units across the neighbourhood.

Every machine that leaves the shop is checked and demonstrated first, and we stand behind what we sell with repair, servicing and AMC support.

Milestone

Now an authorized Jack showroom

We recently opened our exclusive Jack showroom and service centre on 30 Feet Road — stocked directly with Jack machines and genuine spare parts. Jack's own team travelled from China for the opening, and cut the ribbon alongside us.

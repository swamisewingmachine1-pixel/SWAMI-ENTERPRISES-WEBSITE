# Trust Asset Plan

Audited where the site currently relies on JACK's manufacturer imagery
vs real Swami-specific evidence. **35 of 37 product pages use official
JACK catalog photography** (real, but not Swami's own) — only 2 images
site-wide are genuinely Swami's (owner portrait, showroom interior, both
on `/about`). Priority order for real assets, unchanged reasoning from
earlier phases, restated here as the authoritative single list:

1. **Real showroom** — wide shot of the actual sales floor (currently
   only one interior photo exists).
2. **Real machines on Swami's floor** — especially the JACK F6, the
   site's featured product, which currently shows only manufacturer
   photography.
3. **Real machine demonstrations** — ties directly to the video plan
   (`docs/VIDEO_PLAN.md`), videos 1-2 need no new facts, just filming.
4. **Real spare parts** — the site claims "genuine spare parts" (real,
   already-published claim) but shows no photo evidence of stock.
5. **Real team** — beyond the one existing owner photo.
6. **Real installations** — if any past customer installations can be
   photographed with permission.
7. **Real delivery** — trucks/handoff, signals real operating scale.
8. **Authorized-dealer documentation** — a certificate or verifiable
   proof, needed to eventually support the authenticity FAQ content gap
   identified in the topical map.
9. **Real customer stories** — zero exist currently; the case-study
   architecture was deliberately not pre-built (Phase 6) since an empty
   template is dead code, not infrastructure.
10. **Real reviews** — already exist and are real (Google 4.8★/102,
    Justdial 4.2★/135, confirmed via search in Phase 6) — not a gap,
    but worth actively growing and eventually surfacing more visibly on
    the site itself once you're comfortable with how (real reviews only,
    linked to the actual GBP/Justdial listing, never re-typed as static
    site copy where they can't be verified by a visitor).

No fabricated imagery exists anywhere on the site — confirmed by this
audit. The gap is real photos not yet taken, not fake photos to remove.

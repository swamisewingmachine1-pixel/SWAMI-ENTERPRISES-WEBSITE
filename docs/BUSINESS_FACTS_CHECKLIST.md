## E. Second model to compare with JACK F6

**Recommended: JACK 2002G** — already a real product in the catalog
(`Home.dc.html`, `id: 'jack-2002g'`), no new product data invented.

**Why this pairing is genuinely useful, not arbitrary**: both are
computerized lockstitch machines, but positioned differently on real,
already-documented specs:
- **F6**: high-speed general lockstitch, up to 5000 s.p.m., built for
  shirts/wovens/uniforms — the site's featured product.
- **2002G**: large-hook lockstitch, up to 3000 s.p.m. but 12mm max stitch
  length (vs F6's 5mm) and a heavier DPx17 22# needle — positioned for
  shirts/jeans/non-woven bags/shoes, i.e. heavier material than F6
  handles.

This is a real "which one do I actually need" decision (speed vs
material weight), not two near-identical machines being compared for
content's sake.

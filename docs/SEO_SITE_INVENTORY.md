architecture makes that class of bug structurally impossible.

## Product pages (37)

All 37 follow one template (`isProduct` view): H1 = model name, unique
title/description/canonical, `Product` JSON-LD, real specs, real
applications. 34 are real JACK sewing machines with real photography
(mix of official JACK catalog images and 2 genuinely Swami-taken photos
site-wide). 3 (`jack-t3-cutter`, `jack-l5-press`, `jack-tailor-scissors`)
are accessories added Phase 8 with real sourced specs but no photos yet
(honest empty placeholder, not fabricated imagery).

**Found and fixed this phase**: the `/machines` browse page used a
hardcoded 4-category list that silently excluded the 3 new accessory
categories — they were unreachable by browsing (only by direct URL or
site search). Fixed to include any category present in the real catalog.
